## 1-5. Notation and method notes

* Sign convention: deflections are reported positive downwards.
* Shelf model: grillage FE (`calc/grillage.py`), plate bending + twisting
  with folded edge beams; validated in 6.1 against two closed-form plate
  solutions. The model is 8-15 % flexible relative to the closed forms, i.e.
  conservative; no calibration factor is applied.
* Joint model (`calc/joints.py`): clamped friction-grip joint with bolt
  bearing as backstop. Capacities are EC3-style design values
  (0.6/0.9 fu As / 1.25), i.e. *not* ultimate and not a certified rating.
* Racking (`calc/racking.py`): three independent mechanisms, each with its
  own stiffness; the friction-dependent one is also evaluated in the
  'friction lost' state to show why it is required.
* Stability (`calc/stability.py`): masses and centres of mass computed from
  the CAD solids; tipping about the real support polygon.

Load cases on one tray-to-slab joint:

| action | demand | capacity | utilisation |
|---|---|---|---|
| vertical shelf reaction, top tray (15 kg / 2 sides) | 73.6 N | 14.31 kN | 0.51 % |
| pure slip (friction) at 100 N lateral | 100.0 N | 2.70 kN | 3.70 % |
| slot bearing at 100 N lateral | 100.0 N | 19.18 kN | 0.52 % |

Joint moment at first opening: M_sep = N.h/6 = 45.0 N.m per flange; demand 7.62 N.m -> margin 6:1.

Slip margin against the 100 N handling disturbance: 27:1 - the joint stays metal-to-metal, so the frame
behaves as rigid-jointed for racking (see 7.2).

Caveat, stated plainly: this joint's moment capacity is preload-
dependent. If the bolts were ever allowed to slacken completely the
joint would become a pin and the 190 mm speaker bay would rely on the
panel below and on the tray's own plate bending. The maintenance plan
therefore includes a torque check at one week, one month and annually.

Loosening: nylon-insert locknuts (DIN 6923) plus a preloaded clamped
joint; no spring washers, no polymer washers in the structural stack.
Because the joint does not slip in service there is no fretting of the
powder coat at the interface. Torque is checked at assembly and the
locknuts are visible for re-torque during the annual inspection.

## 7. Racking, torsion and lateral stability

### 7.1 Front-to-back racking (Y)

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| side slab shear area | 752 mm2 |
| shear stiffness G A / h | 81.7 kN/mm |
| drift at 100 N | 1.2235 mm |

The side slabs are continuous plates lapped to all three trays; the
load path is in-plane shear, which bolts transmit in bearing. This path
does not rely on friction. Practical drift will be governed by bolt
clearance take-up (0.25 mm) and is capped by that value.

### 7.2 Side-to-side racking (X)

| mechanism | stiffness | drift at 100 N |
|---|---|---|
| rear panel shear diaphragm (400 mm tall) | 210.81 kN/mm | 0.00047 mm |
| side slabs, rigid-jointed 190 mm bay | 0.04 kN/mm | 2.67 mm |
| side slabs, Pinned joints (friction lost) | 0.01 kN/mm | 10.67 mm |

Governing drift with clamped joints: **2.67 mm** at 100 N (criterion 2.0 mm) -> PASS.

If the upper joints were friction-free pins the bay alone would drift
10.67 mm, i.e. it would exceed the criterion. The clamped joint is
therefore a structural requirement, and it is verified: friction capacity
27:1 against the same 100 N (see 5.1), with the bolts in bearing as a
positive backstop for the shear. The rear panel is provided anyway so that
the lower 400 mm of the stand is immune to clamp pressure.

Allowed joint rotation for the 2 mm criterion: theta = 0.0066 rad = 0.38 deg over the bay height.

### 7.3 Torsion (twist about the vertical axis)

The lower bay is a closed box (two side slabs + base tray + rear panel +
middle tray) in which every panel resists in-plane shear, so torsional
stiffness is dominated by the rear panel and the two side slabs acting as a
tube. The upper bay is open at the rear; a torsional disturbance there is
carried by the top tray as a diaphragm (566 x 400 plate clamped on two
sides, see 6.2: 0.3 mm under the design load). Torsional deflection of the
turntable support surface is therefore of the order of 0.1 mm at 10 N.m,
which is far below the 0.5 mm twist criterion used for the turntable.

## 10. What this report does not establish

* No physical test of any kind has been performed. All values are
  analytical (with a self-written FE implementation) plus CAD checks.
* No supplier has reviewed the files, quoted, or accepted them.
* The plywood/steel properties are handbook values, not mill certificates.
* Dynamic behaviour (resonances above ~30 Hz), acoustic isolation and
  long-term creep of the isolation pads are not analysed; they are handled
  by design measures and by the staged home test plan.
* Buckling of the 1.88 mm plates is not governing at the stress levels
  computed (max 47 MPa, t/b ratio 1.88/400); a formal plate buckling check
  would be required for loads several times the design values.
